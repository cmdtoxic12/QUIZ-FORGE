    What the agent actually does

    Five question generators run over your text, then compete for a slot in the final game.

    📖

    Definition mining

    Finds “X is / refers to / means Y” patterns and turns them into concept questions with rival definitions as decoys.

    🧩

    Cloze deletion

    Ranks salient terms by frequency and proper-noun weight, then blanks them out of a real sentence.

    🔢

    Figure hunting

    Grabs statistics, dates and percentages and generates near-miss numeric distractors.

    🕵️

    Truth spotting

    Corrupts sentences by swapping entities or flipping claims — only one option survives verbatim.

    ⚖️

    Fact check rounds

    Fast true/false statements with quiet entity swaps and negations to catch skim-readers.

    🎚️

    Difficulty curve

    Candidates are scored, de-duplicated and interleaved so no two questions feel the same.
